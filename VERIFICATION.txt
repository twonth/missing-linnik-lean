The new targets explicitly have NO UPPER BOUND ON x. They retain the
existing von Mangoldt formulation: the two-term estimate holds for x>=1,
and the three-term estimate for x>=Y=q^(170k). Their quantitative errors
are 10^42 log(Y)log(x)/phi(q) and 10^46 log(Y)log(x)^2/phi(q).
For B=170k and Z=q^(B^2), log(Y)/log(x)<=1/B throughout x>=Z.

not separately packaged prime-only statements for every integer B.

correspondence and limitations are in Verification/manuscript-alignment.md.
