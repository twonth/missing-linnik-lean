NEW RESULTS CHECKED BY LEAN

1. Appendix 1: one-parameter anti-concentration.
   OneParameter.lean, MissingLinnik.CurrentPaper.one_parameter:
   for integers t >= 3 and f >= 2, a primitive real Dirichlet character
   psi of conductor f, and real x >= f^(t^2),

     sum_{p <= x, psi(p) = -1} log(p)/p
       <= (1/2 + 70000/t) log(x).

   This proves the stated O(1/t) assertion with an absolute effective
   constant. The numerical constant is deliberately not optimized.

   LinearTriangle.lean reuses the previous triangular van Dijk
   nonzero-minor, divisibility, and determinant estimates. The old
   final bound had an unnecessarily quadratic error in the slope.
   The revised last estimate has linear error 32Y. OneParameter.lean
   chooses Y = 1024t and handles the prime 2 and real cutoffs explicitly.
   This fixed rescaling is the only change of parameter convention;
   there is still only one freely chosen parameter.

2. Proposition 2 and Theorem 1.
   CurrentPaper.lean establishes effective anti-concentration with
   computable exponent A(k) = (70000(k+1))^2, then the arbitrary-epsilon
   statement and Linnik's theorem. The final theorem now uses the
   triangular van Dijk route, rather than the previous Green route.

   The existing formalization supplies the symmetry-formula argument,
   Parseval and cubic identities, extraction of a real character,
   exclusion of its positive bias, and the concluding contradiction.
   These are proved ingredients, not hypotheses left to the reader.

3. Appendix 2: sharper Selberg sieve.
   SharpSieve.lean proves |sieveWeight P R d| <= 1, hence the L1 bound
   <= R and the progression upper bound with additive error R^2.
   SharpUpper.lean and SharpLower.lean propagate this through both
   sides of the finite sieve. SharpFundamental.lean and SharpRough.lean
   give the rough-number count with relative error

     16 exp(32-h)

   and additive error z^(3h), for integer h >= 17 and z >= max(2,q).
   This replaces the previous formalization's conservative z^(5h).

PROOF CORRESPONDENCE AND SCOPE

This is a formal verification of the mathematical results using the
paper's proof ingredients, not a literal translation of every sentence.
The main proof retains the previous formalization's von Mangoldt weights
until its later removal of proper prime powers. It also retains its
previous, sufficient rough-number constants internally. The sharper
Appendix 2 estimates are separately proved here.

The sieve count uses natural integer parameters and the interval
0 <= n < N. The manuscript uses positive n <= t and asymptotic notation.
The usual endpoint and integer-rounding translations are not packaged
as a separate theorem for arbitrary real t, z, h in this supplement.
The exact formal statements can be read in SharpSieve.lean and
SharpRough.lean. No claim is made to verification of bibliographical
or historical assertions.
